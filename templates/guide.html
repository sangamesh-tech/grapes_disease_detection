General Prevention Strategy

  • 🌞 Site Selection: Choose locations with good sun exposure and air drainage.
  • ✂️ Proper Pruning: Remove excess growth to improve air circulation.
  • 🧹 Sanitation: Destroy diseased leaves, canes, and mummified fruit.
  • 🧬 Resistant Varieties: Use grape varieties resistant to local diseases.

⚫ Black Rot (Guignardia bidwellii) - High Risk

🔍 Symptoms:

  • Yellowish spots on leaves with dark borders
  • Black dots (pycnidia) appear in lesions
  • Grapes turn black, shrivel, and become mummified
  • Shoots show elongated, sunken lesions

💊 Prevention & Treatment:

  • Use fungicides starting at bud break
  • Prune vines to increase sunlight and airflow
  • Destroy infected plant parts
  • Use copper fungicides during dormant season

💧 Downy Mildew (Plasmopara viticola) - Medium Risk

🔍 Symptoms:

  • Yellow, oily spots on top of leaves
  • White downy growth underneath leaves
  • Brown and shriveled berries
  • Leaf drop and shoot dieback

💊 Prevention & Treatment:

  • Use preventive fungicides before wet weather
  • Improve vineyard drainage and airflow
  • Clear vineyard floor of infected debris
  • Use systemic fungicides if infection occurs

⚪ Powdery Mildew (Erysiphe necator) - High Risk

🔍 Symptoms:

  • White powder on leaves, shoots, and berries
  • Leaf curl and yellowing
  • Berries crack and rot
  • Musty odor in infected areas

💊 Prevention & Treatment:

  • Apply sulfur fungicides regularly
  • Ensure airflow via pruning
  • Avoid overhead irrigation
  • Destroy infected material

🔴 Anthracnose (Elsinoë ampelina) - Low Risk

🔍 Symptoms:

  • Circular leaf spots with dark edges
  • Sunken lesions on shoots and canes
  • "Bird’s eye" spots on berries
  • Shoot dieback in severe cases

💊 Prevention & Treatment:

  • Use copper fungicides during dormancy
  • Remove infected canes
  • Improve airflow and reduce humidity

🚨 When to Seek Professional Help

  • Disease spreads to 25%+ of vineyard quickly
  • Symptoms do not match known diseases
  • No improvement after treatment
  • Significant crop loss or vine death
